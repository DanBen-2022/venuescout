Where to find prospects

Bring any of these into VenueScout with Import a list.

Free and public

  • Trade shows: the Javits calendar and each show's exhibitor list or sponsorship brochure (check each site's terms).
  • Nonprofits: gala listings in society calendars and the event pages of NYC foundations and museums; past host venues are often named.
  • Luxury and fashion: NYFW schedules, brand press releases and store-opening announcements.
  • Finance, law, tech, pharma: company newsrooms, conference sponsor lists, and job posts for event managers.
  • Side events: Eventbrite, Luma and Partiful listings tagged to major NYC weeks.
  • Your own records: past inquiries, lost proposals and past clients.

Paid (needed for direct contacts)

  • LinkedIn Sales Navigator PAID: find event and marketing owners by title. Do not scrape LinkedIn.
  • Apollo, ZoomInfo or Cognism PAID: verified emails and direct dials.
  • Clay PAID: AI research that checks whether each company hosted events in the past.
  • SponsorUnited PAID: brands' sponsorship and activation history.
  • Cvent Supplier Network and planner directories PAID: inbound RFPs and planner relationships.

Qualified buyer criteria

Each prospect gets a 1–5 sub-score on past events, event size and budget from the bands below. The rating is the weighted average. A qualified buyer meets the minimum rating and has an event 1–12 months out (or the window you set). Changes rescore every prospect at once and are saved for everyone using this page.
